# Final Project — Proposal

[← Final Project overview](./index.md)

**Due:** Mon 10/26, 11:59 PM

Your proposal is a first draft of the plan you'll firm up at
[Checkpoint 1](./checkpoint-1.md). It doesn't have to be polished or
final — it has to be specific enough that we can give you useful feedback.
Bullet points are fine.

**Submit it to Canvas as a PDF.** Aim for **about a page of text**.
Diagrams or images can take it longer; the text shouldn't.

**Everyone submits on Canvas, but only one PDF per group.** One member
submits the PDF. Everyone else submits a short note listing the group's
members and who submitted the PDF. Working alone? Just submit the PDF.

## What to submit

1. **Your group** — who's in it, or that you're working alone.
2. **An overview** of your app (structure below).
3. **How you'll meet the requirements** (below).
4. *Optional:* **anything you'd like us to relax**, and why — see
   [Required topics](./index.md#required-topics).

## The overview

What the app is and who it's for. This becomes `docs/overview.md` in your
repo at Checkpoint 1 and evolves from there. This is the first draft.

- **One sentence.** What it is, for whom. ("A shared pantry tracker for
  housemates.")
- **Who uses it.** The kinds of people, in plain terms — these become your
  roles.
- **Why it's useful.** The problem it solves, or what it makes easier.
- **Key flows.** Three to five things people do with it, written as "A
  *housemate* can … so that …". These drive your endpoints.
- **What's live.** Your real-time feature: what happens, and who sees it
  happen.
- **Not this semester.** Ideas you're deliberately leaving out.

## How you'll meet the requirements

A sentence or two for each [required topic](./index.md#required-topics),
saying how *your* app will address it.

**Topics we've covered** — be concrete:

- **Data model.** The main collections, and how they relate.
- **REST API.** The main resources your endpoints are organized around.
- **Authentication and roles.** Your roles (at least two), and what one can
  do that another can't.
- **Validation and error handling.** What input could be bad, and what
  should happen when it is.
- **Tests.** The parts of your app that most need testing, and why.

**Topics still to come** — a first idea is enough; "not sure yet" is
fine:

- **Real-time.** Based on "What's live" in your overview.
- **Client.** What kind of client — web, mobile, something else.
- **Deployment.** Nothing needed yet beyond knowing it's required.

## How it's graded

| Requirement | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Group members are clearly indicated | 5 |
| The overview is clear and addresses each of the overview's points | 25 |
| The proposal gives initial ideas on how the project will meet all of the required topics | 20 |
| **Total** | **50** |

## What happens next

We'll respond with feedback. You don't revise the proposal itself — at
[Checkpoint 1](./checkpoint-1.md) the overview moves into your repo as
`docs/overview.md`, and the requirements sketch grows into your other
planning docs.
